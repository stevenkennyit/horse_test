## Rationale (tags: V verified at source, A asserted, NF not found)
**WIN - Crayons In The Sky**
1. She has the strongest form in the race by a long way: the winners of her four races include a filly who was then 2nd in a Group 1 (Haven, V, SL previous_results), another whose runner-up went on to be 2nd in a Group 2 (Livenka's race, V), and an OR 92 winner (Tosca No Candis, V from RP). Her 3/4L defeat by Tosca came at C&D and was the first run at 6f on the Polytrack; today's weight (9-4) matches that run (V).
2. The only runner here with a placing at Dundalk (the others with Dundalk runs, Dragon Scales and Step In Style, were 11th-14th) (V, scanned all fourteen run histories).
3. Never finished worse than 4th in 4 starts (V). In-running shows her always in the first 4 at halfway (V).
4. Drops back to 6f from 7f, where she was 4th and "kept on one pace" (V comment).
5. The field behind is mostly poorly-beaten exposed runners and debutants; only Normanton and Belle are in the same ballpark of being plausible and neither has been placed.
**Case against:** 0 from 4, two seconds and two fourths; "a placer rather than a winner" (playbook Step 2). Her two runs at 6f on Polytrack/turf were beaten by horses carrying 2-8lb less; at Fairyhouse 1 Jul the winner carried 8-11 vs her 9-3 (6lb, about 2.4L), so that defeat is not damning, but she has always been outpaced by the better ones. Thorne is 0-7 in the last 14 days (RP, V). A debutant from a smart yard can still beat her and I could not read the Smart Stats or trainer course records (NF). The time on 15 Aug was slow (1.82s over standard), so the 6f form is not fast.

**EACH-WAY - Belle Parvenue**
1. Drops 8lb from last run (9-2 to 8-8, about 3L at 6f), when she was beaten only 2.35L by a winner at level weights; at Navan she had been at the rear and finished 5th with a clear run (V in-running). 7 days since run, tongue strap added (SL comment, A).
2. Races she was in have been franked by winners: Eyeconic (Navan) and Nation Blaze (Cork) both won since (V).
3. Return to 6f suits a horse who has been "kept on" late (V comment).
4. Place chance on my numbers is stronger than her win chance: she needs only third in a field with five debutants and several 15L+ horses.
**Case against:** never in the frame (5th of 5, 5th of 10); 5.25L behind at Cork with a 12lb weight edge over the winner there (9-4 vs 8-8, so at level weights she would have been about 10L behind that winner), then rated RPR 80 on a narrow defeat in an ordinary maiden won by a 7/2 shot - the playbook's warning about 2yo ratings from narrow defeats. Her best is probably a notch below Crayons; 5f form, dwelt at Cork.

**DANGER - Normanton**: 4th of 13 on debut over 7f in heavy, kept on despite a slow start; likely to improve; Marnane yard is a Dundalk operator (A). Doubts: carries top weight (9-9), 7f heavy to 6f Polytrack, and the Curragh form is untested (NF on the top three), so I rank him below Crayons.
