2. The only runner here with a placing at Dundalk (the others with Dundalk runs, Dragon Scales and Step In Style, were 11th-14th) (V, scanned all fourteen run histories).
3. Never finished worse than 4th in 4 starts (V). In-running shows her always in the first 4 at halfway (V).
4. Drops back to 6f from 7f, where she was 4th and "kept on one pace" (V comment).
5. The field behind is mostly poorly-beaten exposed runners and debutants; only Normanton and Belle are in the same ballpark of being plausible and neither has been placed.
**Case against:** 0 from 4, two seconds and two fourths; "a placer rather than a winner" (playbook Step 2). Her two runs at 6f on Polytrack/turf were beaten by horses carrying 2-8lb less; at Fairyhouse 1 Jul the winner carried 8-11 vs her 9-3 (6lb, about 2.4L), so that defeat is not damning, but she has always been outpaced by the better ones. The time on 15 Aug was slow (1.82s over standard), so the 6f form is not fast.

**EACH-WAY - Belle Parvenue**

**Case against:** never in the frame (5th of 5, 5th of 10); 5.25L behind at Cork with a 12lb weight edge over the winner there (9-4 vs 8-8, so at level weights she would have been about 10L behind that winner), then rated RPR 80 on a narrow defeat in an ordinary maiden won by a 7/2 shot - the playbook's warning about 2yo ratings from narrow defeats.
