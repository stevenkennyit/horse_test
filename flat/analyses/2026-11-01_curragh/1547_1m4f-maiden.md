## Rationale and case against

### WIN — Loosen And Limber

**Reason.** The only runner with proven course-and-distance form this season: 3rd of 7 in a Curragh 1m4f fillies maiden on 20 Oct, beaten 3¾l (and a nose for 2nd) by **Together Now**, an A.P. O'Brien filly sent off 1/5 favourite under Ryan Moore *(verified — result page: Together Now 1/5f won, Sounds Great 2nd 3¾l, Loosen And Limber 3rd 80/1, time 2:38.78)*. She improved sharply from 8th/17 (18¾l) on debut at Galway *(verified — ATR form)*. She is a 3yo filly by Golden Horn, carries only 9-3 — 5lb less than the 3yo geldings and 10lb less than the older geldings — and is with Cromwell, ridden by Gary Carroll.

**Strongest case against.** Two starts only; the good run came in a seven-runner *fillies* maiden, not an open 20-runner field, and she was 80/1 both times, so the market has never rated her highly. Drawn widest of the contenders (18). The improvement could plateau now she faces older colts/geldings; a 3rd beaten 3¾l is a platform, not a win.

### EACH-WAY — Purple Bandit

**Reason.** Debut 2nd of 15 over 1m4f at Roscommon (31 Aug), running on from mid-division to grab 2nd close home, beaten 5½l by Miss Romagna, who made all *(verified — result: Miss Romagna 9-3 made all and won by 5½l, Purple Bandit 9-8 2nd, 28/1)*. Crucially, Purple Bandit finished ~1¾l ahead of **Almazann**, the 4/6 favourite who then won a Curragh 1m4f maiden next time out *(verified — IrishRacing report has Almazann 4th, 0.75l behind the 3rd)*, so the Roscommon 2nd is franked by a subsequent course-and-distance winner. He then failed to stay 2m1f at Thurles (5th/9, "didn't stay" — *verified — ATR form*), so today's drop back to 1m4f is a clear positive. Unexposed Toronado gelding from Jessica Harrington, blinkers retained, at a fair price clearly longer than the Win.

**Strongest case against.** Beaten 5½l by a filly he was conceding 5lb to — on weight-adjusted terms roughly 13lb behind her that day. Only two career starts; the blinkers were already on for the 2nd (no new headgear angle); a debut 2nd in a 15-runner Roscommon maiden can flatter, and Miss Romagna is not a horse who has gone on to pattern class.

### DANGER — Joe Cool

**Why he is the chief threat.** The most proven runner here — OR 75, three flat seconds (plus a flat third) and a hurdle win from 9 starts, cheekpieces working (2nd at Killarney latest, 3l, in first-time cheekpieces — *verified — ATR form*), and 5lb better off with Navy Guitar than when he beat him 6l on 5 Oct *(verified — same Killarney race, Joe Cool 2nd vs Navy Guitar 4th)*. If he stays 1m4f, he beats most of these on consistency.

**Why he is only the danger.** Winless in 8 flat starts (a placer, not a winner), no 12f form — his 13f run was 4th/9 beaten 14½l and his 14f run 10th/13 beaten 14l (both "no extra"/"weakened") — and his latest 2nd came in what winning trainer Liz Doyle called "a very poor race," behind a five-time NH winner (Ballybawn Belter) for whom the trip was "a bit sharp" *(verified — IrishRacing report)*.
