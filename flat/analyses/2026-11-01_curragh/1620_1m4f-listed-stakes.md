## Rationale and case against

### WIN — Thread Of Gold (0.34, fair ~2.9)

**Why:** The most reliable progressive profile in the race, with the top rating (107). His Group 3 Kilternan 5th at Leopardstown over this trip, beaten ~3½ lengths by Arabian Crown, who made all (verified — HRI), is high-class form for a weak Listed, and before that he won back-to-back Premier handicaps — the Nasrullah (Leopardstown 1m1f, off 88, by 1¼L) and the Guinness (Galway 1m4f, off 97, by 1¾L in an 18-runner field) — winning that Galway race on yielding-to-soft, so today's ground holds no fear (verified). He is ~12lb clear of the next in-form horse (The Austrian / Shosholoza / Zadawa all ~94–95) and drops from Group 3 to a weak Listed (€30k, no older male after Factual Fact's withdrawal). Two wins, a 3rd and a G3 5th in his last five runs (plus a 2025 maiden win), and unlike his chief rival he has not run a poor race all season.

**Strongest case against:** 50 days off (his longest gap since his 2yo campaign, though he has had a similar break before). His style is to be held up and produce a late run, so a steadily-run 1m4f on yielding ground could blunt his finishing kick and hand it to a more prominent racer. And the weight-adjusted Kilternan head-to-head actually favours In My Teens, not him: at Leopardstown he carried 9-1 and beat In My Teens (9-6) by only ~½ length (5th beaten ~3½L vs 8th beaten ~4¼L — verified at HRI), so with her 5lb swing at today's level weights she comes out ~2½ lengths better than him on that line, and she also holds one of the strongest performances in the field (Ragusa 7L at this CD). His edge over her is reliability and progression, not superior best form.

### EACH-WAY — The Austrian (0.12, fair ~8.3)

**Why:** The improving runner in the field — form 7211, with back-to-back wins at the trip: a Listowel 1m4f maiden by 2¼L (4/7) and a Thurles 1m4f154y handicap off 89 by 1¼L (13/8) (verified at HRI). Twenty-four days fresh, from a yard (Harrington) whose connections described him as a likely Listed horse next year. In a Listed as weak as this — where the only other recent winners rated below 95 are maiden or amateur-race scorers — a 95-rated, still-learning 3yo with two staying wins is a solid each-way proposition at a much longer fair price than the Win, with the best place chance (~40%) of the outsiders on my numbers.

**Strongest case against:** His wins have come in a maiden and a five-runner handicap off 89, so he still has roughly 10lb+ to find to win at Listed level, and he wandered when hitting the front in the maiden. "Consistent and improving but modest grade" is a place profile more than a win profile — which is exactly why he is the each-way rather than the Win.

### DANGER — In My Teens (0.15, fair ~6.7)

**Why (chief threat):** The horse with the race's two strongest pieces of course-and-distance form. She won the Ragusa Premier Handicap over this CD in June by 7 lengths eased off 96 carrying 9-11, in a time (~2:30.1) about standard for Curragh 1m4f (verified at HRI). And on the weight-adjusted Kilternan line she is the better horse than Thread Of Gold: there she gave him 5lb (9-6 vs 9-1) yet finished only ~½ length behind him (8th beaten ~4¼L vs his 5th beaten ~3½L — verified at HRI), so at today's level weights she comes out ~2½ lengths in front. Today she carries 9-2, i.e. 9lb less than her Ragusa win, and is second-top rated on 106.

**Why not ranked higher:** Her last three runs are 6th of 8 (Goodwood G3), 8th of 10 (Kilternan G3, ran freely), and 12th of 14 (Bluebell Listed, soft, beaten ~13¾L) — a clear decline, and her trainer has called her "talented but not straightforward." The Bluebell flop came on soft, and today is on the yielding/softer side, which is a live doubt. The hood remains on but has not cured the keenness.
