## Rationale

### WIN — Majestic King (8-11, OR 82, draw 9)

**Why:** The weight-adjusted head-to-heads, not rating, make him the pick. On 4 Oct at the Curragh over 1m1f (yielding) he was 3rd, 1¼L behind Iamimmaculate while 3lb better off (8-8 vs 8-11); today he is 6lb better off relative (8-11 vs 9-3), a +3lb swing that narrowly reverses the 1¼L (≈2.2lb) deficit — *verified from Sporting Life result pages for both weights*. He also beat Uncle Goalie by ¾L at Leopardstown (18 Oct) while 3lb better off, on identical terms today — *verified*. He is in the form of his life: 1st (Listowel 1m, 15rn, "comfortably"), 3rd (Curragh 1m1f, overcoming a wide trip), 2nd (Leopardstown 1m100y, nose) in his last four — *verified*. All of that is on yielding/soft, today's ground. 8-11 off 82 is a workable weight.

**Case against:** This is the deepest race of his career — his two wins came at Listowel (off 68) and in a low-grade Gowran handicap, and 82 is his career-high mark in a 22-runner premier handicap, so his ability ceiling may be exposed (*verified: 2 career wins; Listowel win off OR 68*). Draw 9 is neutral, not the soft-ground high draw. A 5yo who has improved 14lb in six weeks can also be peaking; the price of that improvement is now largely spent.

### EACH-WAY — Lady Lilac (9-0 −3lb = 8-11, OR 85, draw 22)

**Why:** A big beneficiary of the weight changes, and drawn 22 (the favoured side on soft ground). She was 5th, ~1.3L behind Iamimmaculate on 4 Oct carrying 9-1; today she carries 8-11 (3lb claim) off the same mark — a +10lb swing vs Iamimmaculate, clearly reversing a ~1.3L (≈2.2lb) deficit — *verified*. She was also 3rd, a short-head behind Diego El Queso over this course and trip (1m, soft-to-heavy) in April while 8lb better off; she is now 17lb better off vs Diego — *verified*. She is a consistent top-five finisher (3rd, 5th, 3rd, 5th, 5th) on soft/yielding at 1m–1m1f.

**Case against:** She has won only once in ~17 career starts (7 places) — a "consistent but modest" placer who is more likely to run into a place than win, which is exactly why she is the Each-Way rather than the Win (*verified: form figures 557305, no win in last 6*). Her Galway 10th (July) shows she can disappoint, and the 4 Oct 5th was a head behind 4th, not a dominant display.

### DANGER — Iamimmaculate (9-3, OR 90, draw 2)

**Why:** The horse who actually won this grade at this track/trip last month (Curragh 1m1f, 4 Oct, off 85) and is progressive (Listowel 2nd before that). If the head-to-head weight logic misfires or Majestic King/Lady Lilac regress, he is the most likely winner. He stays on strongly and the formscan's note that he "might prefer the ground a bit softer" fits today's yielding-to-soft.
