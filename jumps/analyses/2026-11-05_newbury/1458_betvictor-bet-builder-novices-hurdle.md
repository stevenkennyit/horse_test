### EACH-WAY — Grand Teton
**Rationale:** The genuine place proposition at a longer fair price than the Win, and the engine from the outsider check. Unlike the debutants, Grand Teton is a **proven hurdle completer** (two completed runs — jumping risk lower) and a **proven 2m4f stayer** on Good to Soft (*verified*: 6th/11 Ffos Las over 2m4f), so today's 2m2½f on a stiff, galloping track is well within range. Henderson has put up **stable jockey de Boinville** (an upgrade on James Bowen for both prior runs) — a mild intent signal — and the horse is fresh after 207 days. These are reasons **beyond stable alone**: it gets round, it stays, it handles the ground.
**Strongest case against:** The bare form is poor — winless and placeless in two runs, "mid-division, made no impression" last time (*verified*) — and its eye-catching RPR/TS were earned while beaten (a weak signal, Step 8). The place case leans heavily on stamina and connections rather than demonstrated quality, so the floor is a non-threatening midfield run again.
