- **Case against:** he is still a novice who made a visible error late in his only start (greenness), the ground is officially unconfirmed, and newcomer bumpers are high-variance — a well-bred unraced rival from a top yard can beat a short-priced point winner. **EACH-WAY — Geissler (IRE).**

The place probability (0.40) already folds that in. *(verified: 4th in point, hood, pedigree; asserted: price being longer than the danger — market not seen.)*
