## Shape of the race

A wide-open season-opener maiden. **Only two runners have proven hurdle form** (verified from RPR + runs read at source): Moments Away (RPR 128, 2nd on debut) and Fervent (RPR 104, one completion from two runs). The other seven are hurdle debutants off bumper/point/Flat form or returning from long absences. Treat jumping as unproven for the debutants (Step 3.3); lean on bumper/point form, pedigree for stamina and trainer record with newcomers.

**Key verified head-to-head (Step 5/ the cleanest signal):** Moments Away and Merlin Allen met in the **Grade 2 bumper at Aintree (11 Apr 2026, 20 ran, level weights)** — **Merlin Allen finished 3rd, Moments Away 5th, ~4L adrift** of him (in-running read at source). On bumper ability Merlin Allen is the better horse; the open question is that he has **never jumped a hurdle**, whereas Moments Away has (scrappily). That tension is the race.

Newbury is a galloping, stiff, fair left-hander with a long run-in (~1½f / 255–320y per sources) that rewards genuine stayers and sound, bold jumpers — jumping and stamina preferred to raw pace; no clear hurdle pace bias found, treat pace as neutral. No draw factor over jumps. Ground is good-to-soft/soft in places — all the main contenders have form on good-to-soft or easier (verified).

### WIN — Moments Away
- **Reason:** The only runner that has already shown hurdle form *and* translated bumper class to it — RPR 128 (top in the field, **verified**), a 2nd on his hurdle debut over 2m4f (**verified**: Southwell 1 Oct 2026, 2nd of 5, beaten 2½L at 10/11, Class 4), plus placed bumper form up to Grade 2 level. **Proven to stay beyond today's trip and handles good-to-soft/soft** (bumper placings at Chepstow soft and Fairyhouse yielding — verified); drops to a sharper 2m½f with Sean Bowen aboard. In a field where six rivals have never jumped a hurdle, a completed, placed hurdle run is the single most relevant piece of evidence.
- **Case against:** He was **beaten at odds-on last time, "not fluent 7th" and made "no impression"** on the winner (**verified** from the Southwell result) — a jumping lapse *and* no finishing kick; and **he was beaten ~4L by today's rival Merlin Allen in the Grade 2 Aintree bumper** (verified), so he is not even the best bumper horse here. He steps up in class having failed to win a weaker race, and is 0/5. The jumping/kick doubt caps him at 0.30, only narrowly ahead of Merlin Allen.

### EACH-WAY — Fervent
- **Reason:** After Moments Away he has the **best proven hurdle form in the race (RPR 104, verified)** and is a dual Flat (handicap) winner, so ability is not in question; crucially he has **completed a hurdle and "kept on" over 2m** (4th at Worcester, verified), which none of the six debutants can claim, and today's galloping 2m½f should suit a horse that stays rather than quickens. That gives a genuine each-way place floor at a long fair price — a different, longer-priced proposition than the Win, chosen on place expectation (0.33), not as second-favourite.
- **Case against:** The evidence is honestly modest — his completed run was a **4th beaten 8½L by a 4/6 winner**, he **fell (when already weakening/outpaced) on his hurdle debut**, and both his own connections' read and the form say "handicaps more his bag." His place chance leans heavily on the debutants around him failing to fire; hence win only 0.08 and confidence **low**.

### DANGER — Merlin Allen
- **Why:** Has the **strongest single piece of pure form in the field — 3rd of 20 in the Grade 2 Aintree bumper, beaten only ½L into 3rd, and ~4L clear of Moments Away at level weights** (verified head-to-head), after winning on debut and a Kempton 3rd — a progressive, consistent bumper horse, all on today's ground. If that ability transfers over hurdles he wins. He is held just below the Win (0.24 vs 0.30) **only** because he is a hurdle debutant (jumping unproven) returning from 208 days off after a breathing operation, and at Aintree he led then found "no extra" late — three unknowns stacked on top of unproven jumping. A clean round would make this look generous.

## Stress test / key doubts

- **Moments Away:** jumping lapses + no finishing kick on his one hurdle start, now up in class, and already beaten ~4L by Merlin Allen on the bumper book. If the debut scratchiness recurs he is vulnerable to a cleaner jumper; the drop in trip helps only if he jumps fluently enough to use his pace. The Win case is "proven over hurdles" not "best horse" — a genuinely narrow call over Merlin Allen.
- **Fervent:** a single, modest completed run (4th, btn 8½L) is thin, and his other hurdle start was a fall; if he makes an error he is gone. The EW is a place/value call resting on the debutants faltering, hence low confidence.
- **Merlin Allen:** everything hinges on an unproven hurdle round after a 208-day layoff and a wind op — exactly the debutant risk the playbook flags. His superior bumper form is irrelevant if he jumps slowly or green.
- **Whole race:** an open maiden where Merlin Allen, Presley or Youcancountonme could all win on debut; the Win is deliberately held to 0.30 and the top two are near-level.
