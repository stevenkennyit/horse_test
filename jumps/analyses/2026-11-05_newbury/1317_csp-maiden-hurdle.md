## Shape of the race

The other seven are either hurdle debutants off bumper/point/Flat form, or returning from long absences. Treat jumping as unproven for the debutants (Step 3.3); lean on bumper/point form, pedigree for stamina and trainer record with newcomers. Newbury is a galloping, stiff, fair left-hander with a long run-in (~1½f / 255–320y per sources) that rewards genuine stayers and sound, bold jumpers — jumping and stamina preferred to raw pace; no clear hurdle pace bias found, treat pace as neutral. No draw factor over jumps.

- **Aquaman** — German Group 3 Flat ability is real class, but he was **pulled up on his hurdle debut** last month — the jumping question is already answered the wrong way; cold yard (4%).

- **Octo Gane** — "showed ability in bumpers" but modest (4th/6th), 213 days off and a wind op, hurdle debut; "others arrive with more substance" per the card and the data agrees (3%).
- **Military Air** — Frankel-bred and useful in France in 2025, but has struggled in Britain in 2026, is a hurdle debutant for a cold yard, and wears a hood (2%).

### WIN — Moments Away
- **Reason:** The only runner that has already shown hurdle form *and* class — RPR 128 (top in the field, **verified**), a 2nd on his hurdle debut over 2m4f on good ground (**verified**: Southwell 1 Oct 2026, 2nd of 5, beaten 2½L at 10/11), and placed bumper form up to Grade 2 level (5th of 20 G2 Aintree — **verified**). Proven to stay beyond today's trip and acts on good/good-to-soft; drops to a sharper 2m½f with Sean Bowen staying aboard. In a field of debutants, relevant experience and a proven round count for a lot.
- **Case against:** He was **beaten at odds-on last time and the in-running read "not fluent" (twice) and "ran on, no impression"** (**verified** from the Southwell result) — i.e. a jumping lapse *and* no finishing kick. He steps up in class (Class 4 → Class 3) having failed to win a weaker race, and he has yet to win in 5 starts. The jumping doubt caps him (Step 13): 0.32, not higher.

### EACH-WAY — Fervent
- **Reason:** After Moments Away he has the **best proven hurdle form in the race (RPR 104, verified)** and is a dual Flat winner, so ability is not in question; crucially he has **completed a hurdle (4th last time), i.e. jumped round**, which none of the six debutants can claim. At a likely longer price than Presley/Youcancountonme, that gives a genuine each-way place floor (place 0.40). Sam Twiston-Davies and the hood retained.
- **Case against:** He **fell on his hurdle debut** (jumping doubt is on the record), the card reads "may be one for handicaps," and his yard is quiet (Greatrex 1-15 last 14 days). The fall is the reason he is a price, and the reason his win chance stays at 0.10. *(Detail of the two hurdle runs — where/how he fell, the 4th — not yet read at source: see gaps.)*

### DANGER — Merlin Allen

If that ability transfers over hurdles he beats these. He is the chief threat precisely because the Win's edge is experience rather than raw class. Held below the Win only because he is a **hurdle debutant (jumping unproven), returning from 208 days off after a wind operation** — three unknowns stacked together.
