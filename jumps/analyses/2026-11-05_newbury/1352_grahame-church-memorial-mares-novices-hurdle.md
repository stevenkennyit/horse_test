## Rationale and case against

### WIN — Princess Day (0.24 win / 0.55 place)
**Why:**
- **Course-and-distance winner at Newbury over 2m 69y** — exactly today's track and trip (won the OLBG Mares' Open NHF, 28 Feb 2026, 5/2, beat Fairy Park 4¾L). Course form is a genuine edge on a stiff, galloping track, and she has already proven she handles this precise test. *(verified — SL result)*
- **Race-fit (25 days) and consistent:** 1110-2 — three bumper wins from her first starts, then 2nd on hurdle debut. Top RPR in the field (120). *(verified)*
- **The drop to 2m 69y suits.** Her one hurdle run (2nd, Class 3) was over 2m3f, where she went on before 2 out but "ran on one pace" when headed — a trip that stretched her; back at her winning C/D trip the pace question eases. *(verified — Chepstow in-running)*
- Jeremy Scott placed Golden Ace to win the Cheltenham mares' novice — a yard that knows how to campaign a good mare. *(verified)*

**Case against (verified/asserted):**
- **Beaten 3¾L as the 4/6 favourite** on her only hurdle start, headed before the last and finding only one pace — not a dominant performance, and the winner is not in today's field. *(verified)*
- Her proven class is in **bumpers**, not over hurdles; she has one hurdle run. *(verified)*

### DANGER — The Flaggy Shore (0.22 win / 0.52 place)
- **The field's only hurdle winner** (confirmed by the penalty structure), and an impressive one: made all and drew 12L clear, "jumping her hurdles aggressively," in a 2m Class 4 novice hurdle at Ffos Las (20 May 2026). A bold front-running jumper — a profile that suits galloping Newbury. *(verified — RP report)*
- Well-regarded (Cheltenham/Kelso/Newbury spring targets) with Listed-class bumper form behind her. *(verified)*
- **Against:** just one hurdle run, and it was a 5-runner Class 4; she returns off a **169-day layoff** (today is her planned November reappearance — fresh-run record of the small Clarke yard not established) and **concedes 5lb** to every rival as top weight. *(verified layoff/penalty; yard reappearance record not found)*

### EACH-WAY — Connells Cross (0.15 win / 0.48 place)
**Why (best each-way value on my own numbers):**
- At **16/1** she finished 3rd at Chepstow (11 Oct), beaten only 3¼L by the winner and **just ½L behind Princess Day (who was 4/6 favourite)** — a materially better run than her price implied, and she did it despite a jumping error 3 out that she rallied from. *(verified — Chepstow result + in-running comment)*
- **Race-fit (25 days),** stays (3rd at 2m3f) and drops to a sharper 2m½f today; two bumper wins show she is progressive. RPR 119, second-highest in the field. *(verified)*
- She should again be a longer price than Princess Day on the same recent line, making her the better each-way proposition.

**Case against:**
- **Maiden over hurdles** with a recorded jumping error to iron out — novice jumping risk is real on a stiff track. *(verified error 3 out)*
- The Chepstow run still only made 3rd; she has to improve to win and reverse nothing with the fit favourite she was close to.

*(The Danger, The Flaggy Shore, is written up above alongside the Win/EW.)*
