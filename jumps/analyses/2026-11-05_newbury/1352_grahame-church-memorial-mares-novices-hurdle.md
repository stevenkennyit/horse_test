## Outsider check (every runner outside the Win/EW/Danger)

- **Nan's Choice** — the most dangerous of the debutants: a Grade 2 bumper winner for an elite yard with the stable jockey. If she jumps on her first try she can win. Held off the top three only on unproven hurdle jumping and a 210-day absence; comfortably my chief "fourth horse" and a reasonable alternative Danger.
- **Royal Response** — Henderson/de Boinville, £290k ex-pointer who won her Irish point. Classic "could be anything" profile, but nothing proven over hurdles, 221 days off, and the playbook caps unproven potential below strong proven form (which several rivals have).
- **Martini Majesty** — won her bumper debut in Class 2, but the trainer himself says the form "hasn't worked out," and she is a hurdle debutant off 210 days. Not enough to promote.
- **La Trinite** — only fit 4yo with a recent run, but a bare RPR 90 and a single modest 3rd; needs to improve a lot.
- **Gaelic Heather** — lightly raced, beaten favourite last time, 206 days off, no rating to work with.
- **Rosie's Honour** — one bumper 3rd, 218 days off; good yard but no evidence yet.
- **Snow Is In The Sky** — pulled up on both hurdle starts, tailed off; no case to win.

None of these beats a selection; Nan's Choice is the closest and is noted as the alternative threat.

## Rationale and case against

### WIN — The Flaggy Shore (0.27 win / 0.55 place)
**Why:**
- **Best proven hurdle form in the race.** Placed 2nd beaten 1¾L in a **Class 1** novice hurdle at Market Rasen over 2m on good-to-soft, and 6th in another Class 1 (Sandown) — a class above the Class 3 hurdle-debut form the fit pair bring. *(verified — Sky profile + search of RP report)*
- **Proven hurdle winner and a bold, fluent jumper:** led throughout and drew ~12L clear "jumping with real intent" in a 2m novice hurdle, beating bumper graduates (RP report). *(verified via RP report text; exact date/venue to re-confirm)*
- **Running style suits Newbury.** A front-running, sound jumper on a wide, galloping, stiff track that rewards exactly that; front-runners carry a slight edge here. *(asserted — course character verified; her front-running style verified; the combination is inference)*
- Drops into Class 2 and meets rivals who are either exposed at a lower level or unproven over hurdles.

**Case against (verified/asserted):**
- **169-day seasonal reappearance** from a small yard whose fresh-run record I have not established — she may need the run. *(verified layoff; reappearance record not found)*
- **Carries a 5lb penalty (11-5), top weight,** giving weight to every rival. *(verified from card)*
- Her headline Class 1 form is placings, not wins at that level; one of her "wins" may have been a bumper (code of the 12L win to re-confirm). *(asserted)*

### EACH-WAY — Connells Cross (0.15 win / 0.48 place)
**Why (best each-way value on my own numbers):**
- At **16/1** she finished 3rd at Chepstow (11 Oct), beaten only 3¼L by the winner and **just ½L behind Princess Day (who was 4/6 favourite)** — a materially better run than her price implied, and she did it despite a jumping error 3 out that she rallied from. *(verified — Chepstow result + in-running comment)*
- **Race-fit (25 days),** stays (3rd at 2m3f) and drops to a sharper 2m½f today; two bumper wins show she is progressive. RPR 119, second-highest in the field. *(verified)*
- She should again be a longer price than Princess Day on the same recent line, making her the better each-way proposition.

**Case against:**
- **Maiden over hurdles** with a recorded jumping error to iron out — novice jumping risk is real on a stiff track. *(verified error 3 out)*
- The Chepstow run still only made 3rd; she has to improve to win and reverse nothing with the fit favourite she was close to.

### DANGER — Princess Day (0.20 win / 0.53 place)
- **Course-and-distance winner at Newbury over 2m 69y** (bumper, 28 Feb 2026) — genuine course form on a track that rewards it — plus RPR 120 (top) and race-fit (25 days). *(verified — SL result)*
- Chief threat if the top two underperform; the drop to 2m½f addresses her one-paced effort at 2m3f.
- **Against:** beaten 3¾L as the 4/6 favourite on hurdle debut, finding only one pace when headed; her best form is in bumpers, not over hurdles. *(verified)*
