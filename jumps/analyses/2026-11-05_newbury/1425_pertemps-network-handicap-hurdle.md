## Rationale & case against

### WIN — Kilwaughter (win 0.16, place 0.46, medium)
- **Progressive and, crucially, race-fit** — 14 days since a game win at Ffos Las (2m7f191y, Cl3, off 125, "made most, stayed on gamely"). In a field where 12 of 17 are seasonal reappearances (several off 180–570 days), a fit, improving 6yo is a real edge. *(verified — profile run list; days-off computed from profile)*
- **Stays the trip:** 4th at Haydock over 3m58y off 125 (btn 3.25l, "plugged on"), essentially today's distance on a flatter track — he gets 3m. *(verified)*
- **Course form:** won at Newbury (2m4f118y, Cl3 handicap, Feb '26, off 118) — proven round the track. *(verified — note: course, not this distance)*
- **Weights:** carries 11-0 but Stenhouse's **7lb claim leaves him ~10-7**, low in the handicap; only +5lb for the Ffos Las win. *(verified — RP weight before claim)*
- **Connections:** Hobbs & White, a yard that has campaigned him patiently upward. *(asserted)*
- **Case against:** this is a **jump to Class 2 and to the full stiff 3m for the first time**, off a career-high 130; at Haydock he stayed but was one-paced rather than dominant, so a stiffer test might expose a finishing-speed limit. The 14-day turnaround after a hard-fought win is a mild negative, and a 7lb claimer adds a little risk. He is a deserved favourite-type, not a standout — hence 0.16, not higher.

### EACH-WAY — Bill Baxter (win 0.09, place 0.38, medium)
- **The single most relevant form line in the race:** 2nd over this **exact course and distance** (Newbury 3m57y, Cl2 handicap hurdle, 6 Nov 2025) off 126, beaten 1½l and only headed in the final 110 yards after a mistake at the last. He runs off **127** today — 1lb higher than a near-win here. *(verified — profile)*
- **Effectively well in:** Upton's **10lb claim drops him to ~10-1**, among the lightest treated; a genuine stayer getting weight on a track that rewards stamina. *(verified)*
- **Stays and acts on the ground:** won Punchestown 3m (2025), placed off similar marks; handles good-to-soft. *(verified)*
- Chosen as the **Each-Way on expectation**, not as second-favourite: a longer fair price than the Win with a strong place chance from a proven C/D stayer.
- **Case against:** **187 days off** (seasonal return) and age 10; he likes to lead or press and there are several other front-runners (Gowel Road, Firestream, Docpickedme, Western Monkey), so he may not control the race; a **10lb claimer** on a horse that needs a positive, tactical ride is a genuine risk. The weight relief has to outweigh the rider inexperience.

### DANGER — Miami Magic (win 0.13, place 0.40)
- **Well-handicapped progressive** — a 145-rated novice chaser (won Newbury 2m7f easily, 3rd in a Sandown G1) reverting to **handicap hurdles off 138**, with a **first-time wind operation (w1, verified on the RP card)** — a strong jumps positive. *(verified: w1; OR138)*
- **Stays 3m:** he was in contention (3rd) when falling 4 out in a G1 over 3m at Aintree, so stamina is not the question — **jumping is**. *(verified)*
- **Case against:** 209 days off, reverting from a season over fences, and he **fell last time** — a jumping wobble carried back to hurdles, plus first-time-handicap over hurdles and fitness are unknowns. High ceiling, higher variance than the two picks — hence Danger, not Win.
