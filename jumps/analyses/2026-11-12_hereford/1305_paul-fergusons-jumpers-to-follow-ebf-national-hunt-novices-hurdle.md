- **Case against:** he gives 6lb to the geldings and 13lb to the mares; he weakened on the run-in at Cheltenham after contesting the lead and Moscow Star also led last time, so he may not get a soft lead; the hood goes on in a race for the first time (worn to post before, which points to a free-going sort) with a rider who has not ridden him; and the Spanish Darling line says the Worcester bumper form is not far behind him.
