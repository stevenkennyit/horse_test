### 1. Boycetown (0.42, medium)
**For:**
- He has the strongest form in the field by a distance: 4th of 21 in the Cheltenham Champion Bumper, beaten about 3L, and 3rd of 10 in the Punchestown G1, beaten 2.5L, OR 128 (**verified**). No other runner has bumper or hurdle form within about a stone of that. RPRs/ORs elsewhere top out at 115–116 for This Songisforyou, whose hurdle record is error-strewn.
- Soft is not a question: 2nd on Soft to Heavy at Punchestown, beaten 3.5L by It's Only A Game, a later G2 4th (**verified**).
- He tracks the pace, and yesterday's 2m soft hurdles here went to horses on or near the pace (**verified**). Not a keen-goer: no "took keen hold" in his comments.
- Cromwell and Donoghue won here yesterday, and two Cromwell horses have won maiden hurdles straight back from 141–179 days off this month (**verified**, a small sample).
- This rests on more than rating and stable: proven soft-ground form, a pace fit and a fit-fresh pattern.

**Against:**
- He has never jumped a hurdle in public. Point experience is asserted only. In yesterday's mares maiden, Light Up The Dark was "not fluent 2 out" and beaten 6L. She was a Flat 88–89 mare having her first hurdle run (**verified** from her SL profile).
- 19 runners on soft gives plenty of room for interference.
- "Ran green" (Punchestown) and "hung right" (Cheltenham) show some immaturity.
- 180 days off.
- Why it still stands: the form margin is very wide, and the doubt is about jumping technique, not ability or ground. The doubt is priced in at 42%, not odds-on. Step 14's hard cap applies to chase debutants; it is applied here only in spirit, through the probability.
