- **Against:** He is **not a fluent jumper** — "not fluent" recurs in his comments and he has one fall (Cheltenham) and one unseat (Haydock) on the record; a jumping error on a galloping track still costs, and he is a 209-day seasonal reappearance. *Verified* (casualty comments read: "fell next", "unseated rider 11th").

### EACH-WAY — Kap Vert (win 0.14, place 0.40, medium)
- **Reason:** The best each-way value on my own numbers — a progressive **6-year-old second-season chaser** who **won the 16-runner Scottish Grand National over 4m2f on heavy**, "travelling strongly… going best 2 out." His stamina is beyond question for 3m210y, he travels into his races (ideal for a galloping track with a likely gallop up front), and he jumps soundly. As a lower-profile improver up in grade he should be a longer price than his chance, giving genuine each-way expectation. *Verified:* Ayr 4m2f win (profile + in-running); sound completion record; carries 10-12, no claim (Houlihan).
- **Against:** **Up 7lb** and taking a sharp drop in trip from 4m2f — he has yet to prove he is as effective at a "short" 3m, and he has no run at this course. 202 days off. *Verified* (weight/OR from card; trip history from profile).

### DANGER — Here Comes Georgie (win 0.14, place 0.40)

Only the 7lb rise and a hold-up style keep him off the top line rather than on it. Scored as coverage, not staked.
