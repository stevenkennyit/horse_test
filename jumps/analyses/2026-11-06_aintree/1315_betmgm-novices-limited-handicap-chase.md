Reading the actual comments softens the "PU + ran out" figures — the run-out came while going well and the PU followed interference — so it is not clear-cut non-staying, but he "took keen hold" in both 3m runs. Step 9 keen-goer caution: a keen-goer should not head the list in a *weak* pace unless likely to be handy. Here he is a genuinely prominent racer in a small (5-runner) field, so he can bowl along rather than be restrained and picked off — the keenness is a minor flag, not disqualifying. Fencing is sound; not a chase debutant, so the Step 13 cap does not apply. I accept 40% but not higher given the unproven 3m stay and faller risk.
- **Dixie Mafia:** kept as the Each-Way rather than the Win because his overall ability (OR 114, patchy hurdle form) sits below Off The Jury's, but his proven 3m-on-soft-at-the-course stamina and weight concession make his place chance strong at a longer price — the textbook each-way engine here.
