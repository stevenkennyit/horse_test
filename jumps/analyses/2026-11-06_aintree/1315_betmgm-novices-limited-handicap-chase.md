- **Off The Jury:** main doubt is stamina at 3m (never won beyond 2m5f; PU/run-out at 3m over hurdles). It is a specific, recurring question, not a jumping one — his fencing is sound. In a 5-runner race where no rival is clearly more proven over fences, I accept 40% but not higher. Not a chase debutant, so the Step 13 cap does not apply.
- **Dixie Mafia:** kept as the Each-Way rather than the Win because his overall ability (OR 114, patchy hurdle form) sits below Off The Jury's, but his proven 3m-on-soft-at-the-course stamina and weight concession make his place chance strong at a longer price — the textbook each-way engine here.
- **Chase-debut / jumping doubts capped two runners:** Leader Crik (chase debut) and Below The Radar (fell on only chase start) are both held below the top two on recorded jumping/experience doubts, consistent with the Mildmay fence-experience angle and the Galway-25-Oct lesson against overriding a recorded jumping doubt.
- **Is an each-way pick warranted in a 5-runner race (2 places)?** Marginal, but Dixie Mafia offers a genuine place chance at a materially longer price than the Win, so a value each-way call is justified rather than omitted.
