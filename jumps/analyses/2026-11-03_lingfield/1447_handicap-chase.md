## Rationale and case against

**WIN — Away For The Night (0.26 win / 0.60 place).**
- **Verified at source (SL horse API):** four chase starts, all completed — won Plumpton (5 Apr 2026, 2m219y, off 92), 2nd Taunton and 2nd Hereford, and won Worcester (12 Oct 2026, 2m110y, Class 5, off 97, carrying 11-11): "took the lead before 3 out, made a mistake and was headed 2 out, rallied to lead again near the finish." So the "win on return" and the "mistake 2 out" are both confirmed, and 2m is a proven, winning trip.
- He is the **only race-fit runner** (won 22 days ago) in a field where his three main rivals are all off 6–7 months. His prominent, lead-before-3-out style is exactly what a sharp, undulating, prominent-favouring track rewards. Progressive at 7yo (won off 92 and 97).
- **Case against:** he is **up 6lb** (OR 97→103) for a hard-fought win and steps up to Class 4 for the first time off that higher mark; and he made a jumping mistake under pressure last time. If the handicapper has now caught him, or the mistake recurs on quicker, sharper fences, a fitter-finishing class horse beats him. Confidence medium, not high.

**EACH-WAY — Superstylin (0.17 win / 0.55 place).**
- Chosen on place-at-a-price. **Verified Lingfield course-and-distance chase winner** (30 Nov 2023, 1st of 13) with three further course placings (2nd 28 Nov 24, 3rd 20 Feb 25, 2nd 13 Dec 25) — demonstrable form on this specific sharp track. And he **genuinely goes well fresh**: he won Worcester (26 Sep 2025) off a ~5-month break; he is off 193 days now, a profile that suits rather than hinders him. Consistent placer off a workable mark (106).
- **Case against:** a 9yo and thoroughly exposed, so limited upside; he unseated once at Lingfield (18 Dec 2024), a reminder the round is not guaranteed; and he is often held up, which on this track means giving the prominent types first run. A place looks more likely than the win.

**DANGER — Touquet (0.20).** The highest-rated (108) and most consistent profile — back-to-back Huntingdon chase wins then 2nd on both subsequent starts, and crucially **placed twice at Lingfield over this exact 2m chase C&D** (2nd of 9, 3rd of 8). If fit he is arguably the best horse in the race. Held below the Win only because he is **off ~215 days making his first start of the season** on a track that rewards race-fitness — a strong-finishing reappearance that just fails to peg back a fit front-runner is the live risk to the two above.
