## Rationale and case against

### WIN — Tom Taggle (0.40 / fair ~2.5)
In a novices' handicap chase where **three of the four have never jumped a fence under Rules**, Tom Taggle is the one who has — twice, both completed: 4th of 10 beaten 1¾l at Chepstow (2m7f136y, GtS) and 3rd of 9 at Worcester last time out (*verified*: Chepstow result page read in full; Worcester 3rd verified on the SL profile, result page not opened). Over fences the single biggest factor is jumping (Step 3), and he is the **only** runner in the field with a proven, completed chase round — a superlative that survives a scan of all four profiles. He is also race-fit, having run just 21 days ago, versus a 166-day and a ~6-month absentee among the debutants, and he won over 2m7f (15-runner handicap hurdle) so he stays at least that far. He handles the ground (wins/places on Good and Good-to-Soft). The chase-debut cap (Step 13) removes his three rivals from Win contention; he is not himself capped.

**Strongest case against:** 3m1f is 2½ furlongs beyond his only win and further than he has ever been — and at Chepstow over 2m7f he was "ridden three out… outpaced by the leading trio and weakened" (*verified* in-running), which is a genuine stamina warning stepping up in trip. He is also a modest handicapper (one win from twelve) carrying joint-top weight (12-0) off 85. If the race becomes a true stamina test he is vulnerable to the proven stayer. The sharp Plumpton track (less of a galloping test than a park 3m) and his stamina sire (Soldier Of Fortune) are why I still make him the Win, but the trip caps confidence at medium and win chance at 0.40.

### DANGER — Hard Stare (0.22)
The chief threat on ability: he has the best raw form in the race, placed (two 3rds) off 86-88 in competitive 8-runner handicap hurdles, and runs here off 85 on chase debut — a touch below his hurdle mark. Honeyball's yard is adept at improving horses for fences. If he jumps and stays, he is the best-handicapped talent in the field.

**Why not the Win / case against:** chase debut (capped by Step 13), **166 days off**, and — critically — he is a **confirmed hold-up horse** ("held up behind the leaders… ridden before two out, made a mistake and weakened two out" at Ffos Las, *verified*) at a track with a strong front-runner bias, having weakened out of a 2m7f race beaten 6½l. Stepping that profile up to 3m1f over fences first time, fresh from a long break, is a stack of doubts that outweighs his talent for the top spot but still makes him the likeliest to beat the Win.
