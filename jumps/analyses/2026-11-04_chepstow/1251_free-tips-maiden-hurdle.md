### WIN — Red Acres Max
**Rationale.** Among the strongest *verified racecourse* profiles in the race (Cool Customer also won a bumper, in a bigger field): he landed the odds on his second bumper start at Carlisle (led over 2f out, forged away, ridden out — **verified** from the run comment) and was a battling 2nd on Heavy at Fontwell (sustained a duel, headed close home — **verified**), so he both stays and handles testing ground. An Irish point win earlier means stamina for a stiff 2m3f is proven (**asserted**, from commentary). Jamie Snowden carries a hot-trainer flag and readies these types; G Sheehan is a strong booking (**verified** flag/ jockey from card). A £240k purchase whose bumper form is said to have been "boosted since" (**asserted**).
**Case against.** Everything over hurdles is on trust — he is a hurdle debutant, so jumping is unproven; his bumper win came in only a **4-runner** race (modest), and he is 227 days off (first run of the season). A fluffed round or a fitter, equally-talented debutant (New Rules, Cool Customer, Docket Man) beats him. Hence the Win is capped at 0.28, not higher, in a 15-runner race of unproven jumpers.

### EACH-WAY — Cool Customer
**Rationale.** The most consistent and the only **course winner** in the field (won his third bumper here at Chepstow — led over 1f out, ran on well; **verified**), with three placed efforts from three bumpers (2nd, 3rd, 1st — **verified**) on Soft and Good-to-Soft, so proven on today's ground and track. A Soldier Of Fortune gelding should relish the step to 2m3f. At a likely longer price than the two above, his verified consistency makes him a genuine place proposition — the best each-way value on my own numbers.
**Case against.** All his form is Class 5 bumper level and he is 234 days off; the hurdles are an unknown, and a possible conditional claim for Fern O'Brien is unverified. If he is merely a nice-handicap-type rather than a graded prospect, the better-bred debutants outrun him.

### DANGER — New Rules
Paul Nicholls' €105,000, Walk In The Park-sired, unbeaten-in-points gelding, from a yard that readies debut hurdlers and with Sam Twiston-Davies up — the staying pedigree fits 2m3f. The chief threat if the Win misfires; not staked only because an 11-month absence and a single career run leave his current level entirely unproven.
