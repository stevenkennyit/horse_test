## Rationale and case against

### WIN: Silk's Legacy (0.19, low)

**For:**
- **Female line:** the dam, Silk Affair, won the 2009 Fred Winter Juvenile Novices' Handicap Hurdle at Cheltenham, "challenged last, soon led, ran on well" (verified, SL result 52080). That is direct evidence of juvenile-hurdling aptitude in the female line.
- **Trainer evidence:** Elliott won this exact race in 2025 with a debutant, Highland Crystal (verified). He is the only trainer in today's field with a win in the eight Academy hurdles checked (verified), and is 9-48 in the last 14 days (verified).
- **Stamina:** the sire, Shirocco, adds stamina for a stiff 2m2f132y (asserted).

**Against:**
- She is unraced, so her jumping is entirely unknown.
- Elliott's other Academy runners were PU, UR, F, 14th and 3rd (verified). He runs these races with mixed-quality stock, and debut jumping errors are common in the series.
- Her reason rests partly on stable and pedigree, which the playbook treats as weaker than form.
- The two Mullins newcomers are inseparable from her on hard evidence. Mullins' 14-day strike rate (29%) is higher than Elliott's (19%).

### EACH-WAY: Destinedforhistory (0.12, place 0.36, low)

**For:**
- She showed early speed: she led at Cork and was still 3rd, ahead of Why Wait, when she fell 2 out (verified).
- She is the only runner with recorded front-running form (verified, full-field scan of three raced runners). With six debutants a soft lead is plausible (asserted).
- She is the most likely of the raced trio to reach a place standard.

**Against:**
- She "raced freely", was headed after 3 out, and had "no impression" before the fall (verified). Today's trip is about 1f62y longer, with a stiff finish: a real stamina doubt.
- She has no completed run, and the fall came when tired. Under pressure late she could make another error.
- The keen-goer rule (Step 9) is why she is not the Win.

### DANGER: Smokin Aces (0.14, place 0.42)

The Mullins yard is in form (7-24 in 14 days, verified) and runs two newcomers here. Smokin Aces and Glenageary are rated level. Smokin Aces is named as the Danger on the Brian Hayes booking only, as a tie-break; that is asserted as the senior of the two riders, and is not evidence on its own.
