## Method notes
- **Performance figures.** "Perf" below means the weight-adjusted level a horse ran to, in today's scale, worked out through rivals' ORs. The scale is about 1.1lb per length at 2m and 1.2lb at 2m3f+. Every perf figure is asserted: it rests on rivals having run to their marks.
- **"Needs".** "Needs" is the perf a horse must run to at its weight today, after claims: 115 minus the lb it carries below 11-12.
