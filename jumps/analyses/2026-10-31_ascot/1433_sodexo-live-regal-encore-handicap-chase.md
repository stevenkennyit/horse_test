## Rationale — WIN: Viroflay
The race's decisive demand is stamina at ~3m on a stiff, galloping track, and Viroflay is the field's most proven stayer: won at 3m (Kempton, Dec 2025, off 136) and at 3m1f170y (Newton Abbot, May 2026, off 140), plus a 3m 2nd (Kempton, Nov 2025) — 7 career runs at the trip (verified, Sporting Life card). All his best form is on good/good-to-soft ground, matching today's Good (Good to Firm in places). He is consistent (11 of 16 career placed; completed all six recent starts) and won last time out. The 7lb claim brings his 12-0 down to an effective 11-7, running him off about 137 — close to the 136 mark he won the Kempton 3m handicap from, so the +4lb rise for a three-runner Newton Abbot win is effectively cancelled (verified claim; the "OR is inflated" reading is asserted). Head-to-head he was only 2½L behind Califet En Vol at Kempton (Feb) while conceding 4lb; today he is 6lb better off on those terms (asserted, 1lb/length scale) and steps up to his better trip. Ascot's right-handed layout also softens his recorded tendency to jump right (verified comment "jumped right at times").

**Strongest case against:** his handicap wins are all at Class 3 or below, and his only recent Class 1 run (the Topham, Premier Handicap) ended 17th after mistakes at the National fences (verified); his earlier success was in hunter chases for amateur riders. He is ridden by a 7lb amateur — Lucas Murphy, a promising teenager (son of Timmy Murphy) Nicholls is giving "a chance to shine", but with roughly 3 wins from 21 in 2026 and 0 from 9 over fences this season (verified via At The Races/RacingStats247) — and he has been off 171 days (verified). The +4lb rise (140→144) for a three-runner Newton Abbot win also looks generous, though the claim cancels it. The win probability is capped at 0.20 because the class step is real and the field is deep.

## Rationale — EACH-WAY: Your Darling
The best evidence-based place case in the race: he has actually placed in this grade at this track and trip — 3rd of 11 in the Betfair Swinley (Premier Handicap, 2m7f185y, Good to Soft) beaten ~5¼L off 139 (verified), doing Ascot's textbook hold-up "smooth headway 2 out" job. He also ran 2nd at Kempton 3m (Cl2, off 138) and won Ludlow comfortably (Cl3) in March — the form of a solid Class 1/2 staying chaser. He stays 3m and handles good-to-soft. His case for a place (not the win) is what makes him the each-way: proven place form at the level, at a fair price materially longer than the Win's.

**Strongest case against:** he is an 11-year-old carrying top weight 12-0, 5lb higher than his Swinley 3rd, is off 182 days, and his only reappearance in the record (Nov 2025, after a long absence) was a flat 4th of 4 as favourite (verified). The last run is also genuinely poor, not just out of context: he pulled up at Punchestown in the race in which his Swinley conqueror Montregard finished 3rd (verified). His place chance (0.38) is below his bare form precisely because the weight, age and layoff make a flat return a live risk — though the Swinley form is franked (Montregard 3rd in a Listed handicap next time).
