All his best form is on good/good-to-soft ground, matching today's Good (Good to Firm in places). He is consistent (11 of 16 career placed; completed all six recent starts) and won last time out. The 7lb claim brings his 12-0 down to an effective 11-7, running him off about 137 — close to the 136 mark he won the Kempton 3m handicap from, so the +4lb rise for a three-runner Newton Abbot win is effectively cancelled (verified claim; the "OR is inflated" reading is asserted).
