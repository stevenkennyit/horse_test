## Rationale

### WIN — Sportingsilvermine (0.36 win / 0.62 place, fair ~2.78)

The strongest overall profile in the race: proven class combined with current fitness and a yard that specialises in this exact switch. **Verified:** he is a 5yo gelding with flat OR 91 (**verified**, Sporting Life card + ATR form); won the Class 2 Old Newton Cup at Newmarket in July (carrying 8-9, 12/1, "always doing enough") and was 2nd of 10 in the Shergar Cup Challenge at **Ascot** over 1m3f211y in August (**verified**, Sporting Life profile). He stays 12.5f and has won/placed up to 1m4f, with good-to-firm his strongest surface (good record 26:3-4-3, **verified**, Trackside). He is fit — 4th of 8, beaten 1.69L, in a Class 2 Newmarket handicap 28 days ago (**verified**, profile). The decisive angle is connections: **James Owen** has a strong, well-documented record of switching capable Flat stayers to hurdles — his all-time hurdle strike rate is ~21.5% (~19% over the last 12 months) (**verified** via trainer stats), with a string of top Flat recruits winning early in their hurdle careers (East India Dock 2nd in the 2025 Triumph; Burdett Road a Grade 2 juvenile-hurdle winner; Ambiente Friendly, a Derby runner-up, won a novice hurdle; Charles Darnay won the Listed Wensleydale Juvenile on hurdle debut). Hurdle jumping is unproven — that is the one genuine unknown and it caps his win chance and confidence.

**Strongest case against:** he has never jumped a hurdle in a race, and a brilliant Flat horse can simply not take to hurdles; his recent Flat form is also patchy (21st, 11th and 6th mixed among the good runs), and today's 1m7.5f at a stiff Ascot is a shade beyond his proven flat best (~1m4f–1m6f). If he is anything less than fluent, the proven-jumping Babs and the NH-bred No Way Jay both have the tools to beat him.

### EACH-WAY — Babs (0.21 win / 0.48 place, fair ~4.76)

The only proven hurdler in the race, and the most likely to run her race into a place. **Verified:** 4 hurdle starts, 4 completions, 2 wins and a 2nd (beaten 0.75L by Lady Kara, who went on to complete a hat-trick of novice-hurdle wins and was rated 112 by mid-summer); 2/2 over hurdles on good ground; won the Worcester maiden "readily" by 9L; fit after 43 days. Note her hurdle debut was a well-beaten 5th (18L at Warwick) and her two wins have **not** franked well — Genealogy (4-9 fav she beat by 9L) has since flopped, and Yeld To Call / Diamond Grace are still maidens (Little Lady Rock, 3rd to her at Newton Abbot, has run 2nd at Chepstow since — a mild positive). So her claims are about reliability, not the strength of her wins. She is the reliable completer in a field where three of her four rivals are making their hurdle debuts — if any debutant fluffs its jumping, she is in the frame, which is what makes her the best each-way proposition at a longer fair price than the win.

**Strongest case against:** she gives 3-4lb to all of them while stepping up from Class 4 to Class 3, and her last win was a short-head/short-head photo in a slowly-run race (winning time 4:00.32, slow by 13.32s, **verified**, Equibase). If both classier debutants jump round safely, she is most likely the one run out of the top two.

### DANGER — No Way Jay (0.25 win / 0.52 place)

Chief threat if the two market principals under-deliver. **Verified:** 4th of 15 in the Listed Ascot bumper (19 Dec 25) at this exact course and distance, beaten 7.25L by Bass Hunter — who was then 3rd in the 2026 Champion Bumper and won his own hurdle debut — and a short head in front of **Forthfactor**, who went on to win the Grade 2 Aintree bumper and make a 24-length winning hurdle debut at Stratford two days ago (29 Oct 26) (**verified**, published results). That is the strongest single piece of NH-relevant form in the race, and it was produced on this course. He won a bumper on debut at Wincanton (right-handed) and his two best runs are both on right-handed tracks, which suits Ascot; the Tizzard yard and Brendan Powell are both flagged hot (**verified**, card insight). **Case against (why not the win):** 224 days off, first run over hurdles, and his two latest runs were tailed-off efforts where he hung badly (beaten 122.5L and ~26L at Newbury) — enough doubt to keep him behind Sportingsilvermine on the day.
