## Rationale

### WIN — Jet To Vegas (0.42 / fair 2.38)
The only horse with proven, recent chase form **and** Ascot course form over fences. Last time he won at Market Rasen (2m5f89y, off 131, carried 11-13) "always doing enough", and that form is franked: runner-up Fringill Dike won next time out (Market Rasen 17 Oct, 15/8, "easily") — *verified* (Market Rasen result page + multiple sources for the follow-up win). His Ascot chase run (Nov 2025) was 2nd of 3, beaten 3l by Thomas Mor (OR 142) and finishing in front of Lucky Place (OR 153) — *verified* — so he has competed over these fences against far better than today's field. He races off OR 135 but, with Mr Lucas Murphy's 7lb claim, carries 11-2 — effectively a 128 mark, about 7lb well-in, and 11lb lighter than his last winning weight (*verified*: SL card jockey_claim = 7). His best form is in small fields (4-runner win, two 3-runner seconds), and this is a 5-runner field. All five of today's rivals are either chase debutants or, in Merely A Detail's case, rated 20lb+ below him.

**Case against (strongest):** the 7lb claim is taken by an amateur (Mr Lucas Murphy) replacing B S Hughes — a riding downgrade; he has chase errors on his card ("slow 1st" at Market Rasen, "jumped right throughout" at Perth); the Market Rasen win was a weak 4-runner race; and today's 2m2.5f is shorter than his best. None of these outweigh the class/course/weight advantage, but they keep him below ~45%.

### EACH-WAY — Merely A Detail (0.11 win / 0.33 place / fair 9.1)
The best each-way profile on the numbers: 3 chase starts, 3 completions, all in the first three (2 wins, 1 third) — the cleanest chase-jumping record in the field (*verified* from profile run list). She's a progressive 5yo mare, gets in at the bottom of the weights (10-5), 14lb lighter than her last run (11-5), and is from a yard booking a top jockey in J J Burke. In a race with two chase debutants who may not jump fences cleanly, her reliability into the top two at a long fair price is the engine of the each-way call.

**Case against (strongest):** she is the lowest-rated runner (OR 114/117) and needs roughly a 10lb improvement to actually win; her last run was a 6l third behind Pep Talking (OR 117) in a race the winner made all — no frank; and she has never run at Ascot. The place angle, not the win, is the point.

### DANGER — Glynn Brae (0.21 / 0.42)
The chief threat if Jet To Vegas doesn't deliver: top-rated (OR 136), a proven stayer who won at Ascot over 2m5f146y on good ground (*verified*), a front-runner at a course that rewards front-running, and he's from a top chasing yard with the stable jockey up. His one recent blemish (9th at Chepstow) was a keen run in a 17-runner premier handicap on reappearance, where he was hampered 2 out. The doubts — chase debut, a recorded "jumped right" pattern, keen temperament and top weight — are why he is not the Win, but his upside is higher than Dance And Glance's if the jumping holds.
