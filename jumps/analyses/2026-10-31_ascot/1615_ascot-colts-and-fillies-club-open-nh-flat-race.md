## Rationale and strongest case against

### WIN — Lord Churchill (0.30, fair 3.33)

**Why.** The only one of the three penalised winners whose key form is confirmed franked: he beat Posh Chick (7½L) and the 11/10 favourite Beau Speed (9L into 3rd) in a truly-run Wincanton bumper, and Posh Chick has since won a Plumpton mares' bumper (Evens, made all) [verified — Posh Chick result]. The two behind Saint Argente (Ivor Bear, Aribidia) and behind Shane's Spirit (Kap In Hand, Company Charge) have all remained maidens [verified — each horse's record checked], so his is the one debut that has worked out. He "kept on well" from a prominent position in a race run at an honest gallop, which maps well onto stiff, galloping Ascot. His on-the-day RPR of 104 has since been revised to 113, the highest in the race, after the form was boosted [verified — RP profile shows 113; Wincanton result showed 104 on the day]. Sporting Life and Racing Post verdicts both select him [verified].

**Strongest case against.** He has changed yards since that win (Seamus Mullins → Nick Scholfield) and makes his stable debut off 213 days, stepping up from Class 5 to Class 2. His new yard is in a cold spell — 0/14 in the last 14 days [verified — RP] — with a modest Ascot record (3 runs, 0 wins, 1 place) [aggregator, not primary] and jockey Tom Cannon is 1/12 in the last fortnight [verified — RP]. The RPR 113 is a reassessment, not what he ran to on the day (104 — level with Saint Argente's 105), and the frank came in a modest mares' bumper while the 11/10F he beat (Beau Speed) is still a maiden. His only run was over 15f, half a furlong shorter than today on a stiffer track.

### EACH-WAY — Shane's Spirit (0.15, fair 6.67)

**Why.** The only race-fit runner in the field (41 days; every other runner is off 213+ days or unraced) [verified — SL card days-since-run]. He is a proven Rules bumper winner (Plumpton, Good to Firm, made all, "kept on well", RPR 94, improving from an 85 debut at Perth where he was 5th of 11 beaten 2¾L) [verified — RP results]. A front-runner who makes all is a genuine edge on Ascot's galloping, undulating track, and in a field where only three or four horses are realistic win candidates he holds a solid place chance at a fair price well longer than the top two.

**Strongest case against.** The 4lb penalty puts him on level weights (11-5) with Lord Churchill and Saint Argente, who are rated ~10lb his superior (113/105 vs 94), and his Plumpton win came in a 6-runner Class 5 race over modest opposition — the 2nd (Kap In Hand, one run) and 3rd (Company Charge, 4 runs 0 wins) have both lost since [verified]. He is a recorded keen-goer ("took keen hold, made all"), so if he is not allowed an easy lead he could pull his chance away — and two genuinely-run-race rivals are in the field.

### DANGER — Saint Argente (0.26, fair 3.85)

**Why.** The strongest raw piece of form in the race on the day: he beat Ivor Bear (2/1F, who had been 4th of 16 in a Cheltenham Listed bumper and 2nd at Doncaster) by 2L at Warwick over 2m — a stiff track and a trip that both point to Ascot [verified — RP Warwick result; Ivor Bear form]. Warren Greatrex (5/22 last 30 days) keeps him, top jockey Sam Twiston-Davies is now booked (James Bowen rode him on debut), and he has had a breathing operation since last run — a known improver angle [verified — RP form comment states it; Greatrex is a documented wind-op advocate, but no horse-specific confirmation was found].

**Case against.** His form is not franked — Ivor Bear is still a maiden (2nd in a Market Rasen maiden hurdle on 17 Oct) and Aribidia (5th Kempton before, 3rd Warwick) is also still a maiden [verified — records checked]. He won on Good to Soft and is untested on Good to Firm; he was held up in rear and "drifted right" under pressure; and he reappears after 219 days following wind surgery, which is a mild unknown.
