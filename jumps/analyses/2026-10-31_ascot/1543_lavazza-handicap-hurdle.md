## Rationale and case against

### WIN — Trustintimes (0.18)

**Reason:** the progressive, unexposed profile the playbook prefers as a win candidate. He is unbeaten in his four hurdle completions at 2m or shorter — wins at Uttoxeter (x2), a course-and-distance win here at Ascot (21 Nov 2025, off 119), and a 2nd at Kempton off 125 *(verified — Sporting Life profile run list)*. That C&D win makes him the only horse in the field to have won at Ascot *(verified — full-field scan of Ascot runs; ATR course record 1-1-0)*. At The Races Smart Stats put him 5-4-1 (80% win) at ~2m and 5-4-1 on similar going, with a 44% yard figure for Fry in the sample *(verified — ATR racecard)*. On the 27 Dec 2025 Kempton form he finished 3.5L behind Williethebuilder while carrying 6lb more, and the weight swing to today is about 16lb in his favour (Williethebuilder now gives him 10lb versus carrying 6lb less that day) *(verified — Sporting Life + IrishRacing head-to-head)*. He also represents a top yard (Harry Fry) with a 17-day turnaround after a flat spin. His only poor hurdle run was over 2m4f in the Aintree Premier Handicap (17th) — a trip he has not proven, not a contradiction of his 2m form.

**Case against:** every one of his hurdle wins came in 7-to-9-runner Class 3/4 contests, and the Ascot win was at 20/1 having been "left in disputed lead 4th" — he may have been gifted the lead. Today is a 16-runner Class 2 off 127, 8lb above his C&D winning mark, and his one genuine big-field test (Aintree) was poor, trip notwithstanding. The flat maiden run last time (4/14 Nottingham) is an unusual prep and leaves a small question over where the yard's priority lies. *(verified — profile/result pages; the "soft wins" reading is my inference.)*

### EACH-WAY — Moveit Like Minnie (0.13)

**Reason:** the strongest course record in the race — 8 hurdle starts at Ascot with six top-4 finishes, including 3rd in this exact race (the Lavazza) last year off 118, a neck behind Indemnity and ~3l behind the winner Alexei *(verified — Sporting Life result 887387 + profile)*. That form is now franked twice over: Alexei went on to win the Greatwood Handicap Hurdle and the Grade 2 Kingwell Hurdle, so a 3rd behind him is genuine Class 2 form. He carries 10-7 today versus 10-9 then, so he is 2lb better in than last year's 3rd, and he is ~14lb better off with Indemnity (last year's 2nd) on that line. He is a reliable hurdler — his two run-outs and two unseats all came over fences, and over hurdles he has 30 completions from 31 starts *(verified — profile, casualties read individually)*. At The Races gives him the best expert rating in the field (145) and a course record of 8 runs / 0 wins / 4 places — the definition of the each-way profile. He is a front-runner/prominent racer, which Ascot's galloping track rewards, and the booking of N de Boinville (rather than a conditional) is a positive signal.

**Case against:** he is a 9-year-old serial placer who has never won at Ascot C&D (3rd/4th/5th there), and his last win was March. He will also face company for the lead from Gower Prince, Nap Hand, Brendas Asking and Rightsotom, which could blunt the front-running edge and set the race up for a closer. His yard (Twiston-Davies) is cold at 1/19 this season. *(verified — profile; pace read from ride descriptions.)*

### DANGER — Morning Mayhem (0.15)

**Why he is the chief threat:** the most consistent current Class 2 form in the field. He was 2nd of 17 in the Welsh Champion Hurdle (Chepstow, 9 Oct) behind Pioneer Pete — a progressive horse winning his 4th in 5 — and 2nd by a head at Market Rasen (26 Sep) to the 2/1 favourite Un Sens A La Vie (off 128), a horse since switched to chasing; both conquerors are franked *(verified — result pages + follow-up checks)*. He stays 2m (won Cartmel 2m1f off 118) and is a mid-division stalker who should avoid a contested early gallop; ATR notes "didn't stay 2m4f, drop in trip a plus", supporting the read of his Cheltenham 6th. Cheekpieces are retained.

**Why not the Win:** he is 0-for-5 in Class 2 (two places), is now off a career-high 128, has never run at Ascot, and his Cheltenham 6th (only run off 128) was below par, even allowing for the 2m3f trip.
