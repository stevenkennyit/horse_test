**For:** He's one of only two runners proven to stay this trip over fences, and his win came on today's ground: he won a 2m7f177y novice handicap chase on good to soft, soft in places, at Ffos Las 14 Oct (verified). He jumped assuredly apart from a peck at the last (SL and RP comments, verified; "assured" is Timeform, asserted). His last two runs are his best (¾L 2nd at Fontwell, then the chase win: verified). He's the fittest runner, 29 days since his last run, where three of the other five have been off 184–240 days (verified). He races prominently and led at Ffos Las, which suits a sharp right-handed track (track character asserted). He carries bottom weight, 11-2, getting 6lb in effect from Catwalk Girl and 8–9lb from the two higher-rated debutants (verified). The two runners with a recorded chase completion at the trip are him and Catwalk Girl; the other four are chase debutants or failed at the trip (verified scan).
**Against:** The Ffos Las form is weak. Second home was an OR88 15-race maiden getting 7lb. Wannabe (OR95, same weight) was "in touch with winner" when he unseated at the last, so the 4¾L margin flatters him (verified). On beaten lengths he ran to about 100, and he's now 101. His hurdle form is exposed and modest: beaten ¾L by an OR80 off 92, and weakened at 2m7f198y at Taunton. That's one chase run, and it ended with a peck at the last. **Holds** as the most likely winner because no rival has better proven chase form at the trip and fitness. Medium confidence, not high.

### EACH-WAY: Catwalk Girl
**For:** She's the other proven chase-trip winner: 12L at Huntingdon over 2m7f129y in May (verified), and she's a 3m point winner (ATR, asserted). She's the likeliest leader, with ATR's top early-pace bar (90%; she led or raced prominently in all six starts, verified), on a sharp track that favours handy types. The 3lb claim (RP, verified) brings her to 11-8 off 110, so in effect she carries less than her rating implies against the debutants rated 109–110. All three of her right-handed starts came to a win or a 3rd, ignoring the Ludlow fall. On her one bad left-handed run (Southwell) her jockey reported she hung right (RP, verified; track directions asserted).
**Prices check:** 0.20 is about 1.23 times the market's 16.2%, just inside the 1.25 threshold. The named reasons are the claim, the pace setup and her proven trip form.
**Against:** She's been off 184 days with a small yard, and its record with horses returning from a break wasn't found. The Huntingdon race was weak: 4 ran, the favourite fell at the 4th, and the placed horses haven't won since (verified). She fell over hurdles while leading (Ludlow 9th, "not fluent", verified), and a front-runner who makes errors over fences is a completion risk. Her best form is on good ground and she was below par on soft twice (verified), though today is good to soft, good in places. On my numbers the each-way is only about break-even at 9/2 with 2 places at 1/4, hence **low** confidence. She's still the best each-way proposition in the field; Firm But Fair, Western George and Another High Five all work out worse.

### DANGER: Chemoulin
His Haydock maiden form (beat OR115 Ballyeaston, who has won since, 2L at level weights; the third has since risen to 121) reads better than anything else in the field (verified). But Solid Performer fell 2 out while going clear, so the form is flattered. He has never run beyond 2m6f, where he weakened towards the finish (verified). He made hurdling errors in three of his four runs, and this is his chase debut after 226 days. The playbook's chase-debutant cap rules him out as the Win.
