## Rationale and the case against

### WIN: Ballydine Boy

- **For:** His race RPR is the best of any runner's single-run figure: RPR 110 in both bumpers (verified, RP; next best are Northern Olympian's 104 over hurdles and Equi Libre's 91 in a bumper). He earned it in a 6½L 4th of 8 in a Clonmel Pro/Am (ahead of the Elliott favourite) and a made-all Worcester win (verified, SL/RP results). He has the strongest pace fit in a race ATR forecasts as weakly run: his pace bar of 90% is the highest, he made all last time, and Market Rasen is sharp, which favours prominent racers (pace verified at ATR; the course bias is asserted). He has jumped round two points over fences, winning one, so jumping is not wholly new (verified, RP comments only). He stays further (a 3m point) and handles soft (verified). O Murphy and S Bowen won this C&D maiden with Moore Brion 18 days ago (verified, SL result).
- **Against:** RP's card RPR column (an adjusted figure, about 10–13lb above each runner's best race hurdle RPR) puts **Northern Olympian top on 117** and leaves Ballydine Boy unrated ("-"). Bumper and hurdle figures are not like for like. He is making his hurdling debut, so his speed over hurdles is unproven, and the one point error was a bad mistake 2 out ("b mstk 2out", RP). The Worcester bumper was a 6-runner Class 5 for conditionals and amateurs that nobody has franked yet. He got a 3lb claim there (10-13), and today Bowen rides at level weights. Northern Olympian already has a well-advertised hurdle run and a top-yard jockey. The points are on RP only and could not be opened at source (not found on SL). At 4/5 the market already rates him about 46%.

### EACH-WAY: Yafaarr

- **For:** He is a Flat OR 74 handicapper with three wins since June, and his autumn form is solid (verified, SL/RP). His yard's comparable ex-Flat 4yo, Military Cross (Flat OR 76–81), went straight to hurdles OR105 and was beaten only ½L at this C&D on 25 Oct, ridden by today's jockey, J England (verified, SL profile and result). That level would make Yafaarr the clear third on figures behind the top two (Show Your Hand about 94–96). The ground (GS, dry forecast) should suit better than the Heavy he failed on last time, and a sharp 2m track helps a speed-based Flat horse.
- **Against:** He has **never jumped in public** (verified: no jumps runs on SL's 25-run career), and his schooling is not found. Stamina is a real doubt: his wins are at 1m–1m2f. He is a hold-up horse who often starts slowly, in a weak pace on a sharp track. The cheekpieces he wore for both autumn wins are off today (RP/SL card show "t" only). ATR's comment is negative ("likely up against it on hurdles debut"). His tongue-tie is not new (RP: t/tp since Aug; SL: t since Apr 2026), so ATR's "first time" note is wrong or means first time over hurdles.

### DANGER: Northern Olympian

- His hurdle form has been franked more than any rival's (the only other hurdle lines in the field are 29L+ defeats or 2m4f): ½L behind Cowboy Casanova (who has won since) and 1½L ahead of Murphy's 5/6 favourite Lexington Wood (verified). A second run for Skelton should bring improvement (asserted). He is not the Win because he was keen in all 4 RP runs with a comment (Munich has none), including "ran too free" at Worcester, and holds up. Playbook Step 9 says not to make a recorded keen-goer first choice in a weak pace unless he is the likely leader, which he is not (pace bar 20%). He was also beaten 7½L on his only hurdle start.
