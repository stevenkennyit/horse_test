Selection value — win or place return

Every named selection (Win, Each-Way and Danger) scored as the SP return of a 1pt bet: a winner returns its full win odds, a placer returns the place fraction of the odds (¼ for 2-place races and 12+ runner handicaps, else ⅕), anything else −1. So a win outranks a place at the same price, but a big-priced place can outrank a short-priced win (25/1 placed = +6.25 beats 9/2 won = +4.50). Win-only fields (under 5 runners) score the winner at full odds. A selection-quality lens, separate from the staked pick-score. Bars are the picks that returned, biggest first; hover for detail.

Data: jumps/data/results.js and flat/data/results.js. Add each new meeting to the right file; the dashboard recalculates on reload. Playbook rule changes are logged in data/changelog.js and tracked under "Playbook changes". Selections are Win, Each-Way and Danger. "Placed" uses standard each-way places: 2 for 5–7 runners, 3 for 8+, 4 for handicaps of 16+, winner only under 5. Pick score (at SP): the Win is scored as a €1 win bet — €1 staked (wins → +odds, else −1); the Each-Way as a €1 each-way bet — €2 staked, €1 to win + €1 to place (winner → +odds + odds×place-fraction; placed not won → odds×place-fraction − 1; unplaced → −2; place pays a quarter of the odds in 5–7 runner races and handicaps of 12+, otherwise a fifth). So a full race stakes €1 + €2 = €3 when both are issued, and ROI uses the stake, not the number of bets. The Danger is not staked. Fair price = 1 ÷ the agent's win chance. Selection value is a separate diagnostic, not part of the pick score or the money won/lost: every named selection — the Danger included — is scored as the SP return of a 1pt bet (winner → full win odds, placer → the place fraction of the odds, unplaced → −1; win-only fields under 5 runners score the winner at full odds), to credit finding winners and big-priced placers whatever slot they were given. A win outranks a place at the same price, but a big-priced place can outrank a short-priced win. SP is unknown before the off, so it cannot be gamed at pick time. Legacy meetings (before 27 Oct 2026) had no explicit roles; the dashboard maps their rank 1 → Win, rank 2 → Each-Way, danger → Danger, so their scores are recalculated under the new model.
